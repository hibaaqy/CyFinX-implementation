Task: Final investigation report.

Produce a concise structured investigation report from the supplied case-time evidence.
State the conclusion, confidence, supporting observations, limitations, and unresolved questions.
Do not present allegation or hypothesis as established fact.
Do not use a ground-truth label or scenario name.
Each supporting observation must include at least one evidence reference.
Respond only with the JSON object defined by report_schema.json.

Evidence references must be record identifiers copied exactly from the evidence JSON: the string value of an identifier field such as Transaction_ID, Auth_event_ID, Alert_ID, Session_ID, Device_ID or Beneficiary_ID. Cite the identifier value only, never field names, timestamps, amounts or record text, and never several fields in one string. Do not cite Case_ID, Customer_ID or Receiver_ID values. Cite only the few records that directly support a finding (usually 1 to 5 per list). Do not list records merely because they exist, and never write out runs of consecutive identifiers.
